# Platform Adaptation Prompt – Task 2

## Objective
Rewrite an existing UGC ad script for multiple platforms while maintaining core message and CTA.

## Context
Business: AI Automation Services (AutomateVizag)
Offer: Free 15-Minute Automation Audit
Target Audience: Small business owners in Visakhapatnam

## Instructions

Take the original UGC script and adapt it for:

1. Instagram Reels
   - Fast-paced
   - Strong hook
   - Emotional
   - 25–30 seconds
   - Short sentences

2. Facebook Ad
   - Slightly detailed
   - Trust-building
   - 40–60 seconds
   - Clear benefits

3. YouTube Shorts
   - High energy
   - Very strong hook
   - Quick problem-solution format
   - 20–30 seconds

4. WhatsApp Status Ad
   - Very short
   - Direct
   - 3–5 lines
   - Clear CTA

Keep CTA consistent:
“Book Free 15-Minute Automation Audit.”

Do not sound corporate.
Keep tone authentic and human.
