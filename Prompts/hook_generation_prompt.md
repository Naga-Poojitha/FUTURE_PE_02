# Hook Generation Prompt – UGC Ads

## Objective
Generate high-converting, scroll-stopping hooks for short-form UGC ads.

## Context
Business: AI Automation Services for Small Businesses (Visakhapatnam)
Target Audience: Small business owners, clinic owners, service providers, SMEs
Main Pain Points:
- Too much manual work
- Missed leads
- Slow responses
- Disconnected tools
- Operational overload

## Instructions for AI

Generate 15 short, scroll-stopping hooks for Instagram Reels / Facebook Ads / YouTube Shorts.

Hooks must:
- Be under 12 words
- Feel natural and conversational
- Focus on pain or curiosity
- Sound like a real business owner speaking
- Avoid technical jargon

Use different hook styles:
- Problem-based
- Question-based
- Shocking statement
- Relatable frustration
- Results-driven

Do NOT sound corporate.
Do NOT sound like a traditional ad.
Make it authentic and human.

Return output as a clean numbered list.
