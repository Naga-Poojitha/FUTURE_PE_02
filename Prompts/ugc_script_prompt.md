# UGC Script Generation Prompt – Task 2

## Objective
Generate a high-converting UGC-style ad script for AI Automation Services.

## Business Context
Business Name: AutomateVizag
Location: Visakhapatnam
Target Audience:
- Small business owners
- Clinic owners
- Service providers
- SMEs struggling with manual processes

Primary Offer:
Free 15-Minute Automation Audit

## Instructions for AI

Using one selected hook from the hooks list, generate a 30–45 second UGC-style ad script.

Structure must follow:

1. Hook (scroll-stopping opening line)
2. Relatable Problem
3. Personal realization or frustration
4. Introduction of automation as solution
5. Specific benefit (time saved, faster replies, fewer missed leads)
6. Clear CTA (Book Free Automation Audit)

Tone:
- Conversational
- Authentic
- Slightly emotional
- Not corporate
- Not technical

Style:
- Feels like a real business owner speaking
- Short sentences
- Natural flow
- Suitable for Instagram Reels

Do NOT sound like a traditional advertisement.
Keep it realistic and relatable.
